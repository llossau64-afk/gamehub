# Credits — DOWNFALL: NO BRAKES art

Everything in `Art/` is converted from the browser game ONE ROAD DOWN (`OneRoadDown/`). Almost all of it is
generated by that project's own code and needs no attribution:

- `Models/Cars`, `Models/Nature`, `Models/Buildings`, `Models/Garage`, `Models/Animals/{deer,cow,sheep,boar,ibex}`
  and their textures: made by `OneRoadDown/tools/blender/*.py`, converted to FBX/PNG.
- `Textures/Terrain`: made by `OneRoadDown/tools/blender/textures.py`.
- `Textures/Generated`: baked from `OneRoadDown/src/gfx/textures.js`, `materials.js`, `models/circuit.js`,
  `world/world.js`, `scenes/garage.js` (canvas drawing code).

sphere removed and the fridge door animation dropped.
